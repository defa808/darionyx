1. Introduction

Pocket Psychologist ("we", "our", or "us", the "app") is a self-help cognitive behavioural therapy (CBT) application with an AI coach that runs entirely on your own device. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

Our commitment: The app is private by construction. It has no user accounts, requires no sign-up, and does not collect, transmit, or store any of your personal data on any server. Everything you write stays on your device.

2. Who We Are

The app is developed by an individual developer operating under the "Darionyx" name. For any privacy-related inquiry, contact us at:

Data processing: On your device only — no servers involved.

3. Data We Collect

None. Pocket Psychologist collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no tracking.

3.1 Your content stays on the device

The thoughts, notes, cognitive-distortion tags, coach conversations, and belief ratings you create are stored only in local storage on your device. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

Data Where it lives Leaves the device?
Thoughts & notesLocal device storageNo
Tagged cognitive distortionsLocal device storageNo
Coach chat / “Fight Back” messagesLocal device storageNo
Belief ratings & emotionsLocal device storageNo

3.2 On-device AI

The AI coach is a small language model that runs directly on your device. All inference happens locally. Your text is never sent to us or any third party for processing, and is never used to train any model.

3.3 Network use

The only network request the app makes is a one-time download of the AI model file over HTTPS from a public host. This download contains no personal data. Once the model is downloaded, the app is fully functional offline (airplane mode).

3.4 What we do NOT collect

  • No account, email, or name (there is no sign-up)
  • No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking
  • No advertising identifiers or ad SDKs
  • No location, contacts, or biometric data
  • No data from anyone — because nothing is sent off the device

4. Data Sharing

We share no data with anyone, because we collect none. There are no third-party analytics providers, advertisers, or backend servers that receive your information. If you choose to export or share a note yourself (for example, with your therapist), that action is entirely under your control and initiated by you.

5. Purchases

The core app is free. If an optional one-time "Pro" unlock is offered, the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing — we never see or store your payment details. Your entitlement is stored locally on your device. See Google's Privacy Policy for how Google handles payments.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, you are in full control of it. Deleting the app permanently removes all of your data. There is no server copy for us to retain or delete, and nothing to request from us.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, portability, and objection. Because Pocket Psychologist holds no personal data about you on any server, these rights are satisfied directly on your device:

  • Access & portability: all your data is already on your device, visible in the app.
  • Rectification: edit or delete any note directly in the app.
  • Erasure: delete a note, or uninstall the app to remove everything.
  • Objection / restriction: there is no processing to object to — nothing leaves your device.
Questions? Contact defalex808@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 30 days (GDPR Article 12).

8. Children's Privacy

Pocket Psychologist is intended for adults (18+) and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone — and technically cannot, since nothing leaves the device.
