🗑️ Delete Your Account

You can delete your Prostir account at any time. This page explains how to request deletion and what happens to your data.

Option 1 — Delete from the App

The fastest way to delete your account is directly within the Prostir app:

⚙️ Settings › Profile › Delete Account › Confirm
  • 1
    Open the app and go to Settings Tap the Settings icon in the bottom navigation bar
  • 2
    Tap "Profile" Your account information is shown here
  • 3
    Tap "Delete Account" Located at the bottom of the Profile screen
  • 4
    Confirm deletion Read the confirmation dialog and tap "Delete" to proceed. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Option 2 — Request by Email

If you cannot access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us directly:

📧 Email: defalex808@gmail.com

📝 Subject: Account Deletion Request — Prostir

✏️ Include: The email address linked to your Google account used to sign in

⏱️ Response time: Within 30 days (GDPR Article 12)

What Happens to Your Data

⚠️ Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Once confirmed, your personal data is immediately anonymised and cannot be recovered.
Data Action Details
Email address Deleted Permanently overwritten with an anonymised placeholder
Display name Deleted Permanently overwritten; shown as "Former Partner" to your household member
Google account link Deleted Google OAuth relationship is revoked immediately
Push notification token (FCM) Deleted Device token removed; no further notifications sent
Bank account links Deleted All linked bank accounts unlinked; API credentials deleted from Azure Key Vault
Transaction history Anonymised & Retained Your transactions remain in the household for your partner's budget continuity, but are permanently unlinked from your identity and attributed to "Former Partner"
Expense plans & categories Retained Shared household data remains accessible to your partner
✅ No personally identifiable data is retained after deletion. All remaining data is fully anonymised in compliance with GDPR Article 17.

Your GDPR Rights

As a user in the European Economic Area, you have the right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") under GDPR Article 17. We honour all deletion requests within 30 days.

For more information about how we handle your data, please read our Privacy Policy.
