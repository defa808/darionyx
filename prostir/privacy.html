4. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing the service: Importing and displaying your financial transactions, calculating budget summaries, and showing planned vs. actual spending
  • Notifications: Sending push notifications when your spending approaches a budget threshold you have configured
  • Category learning: Remembering your transaction category choices to automatically categorize similar future transactions
  • Bank synchronisation: Periodically fetching new transactions from your linked bank accounts via their official APIs
  • Security: Verifying your identity and protecting your account from unauthorized access

5. Data Storage & Security

5.1 Storage Location

All personal data is stored exclusively on Microsoft Azure infrastructure in the West Europe region (Amsterdam, Netherlands) — within the European Union. This ensures compliance with GDPR data residency requirements.

5.2 Security Measures

  • In transit: All communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • At rest: The database is encrypted using Azure SQL Transparent Data Encryption (TDE)
  • Bank credentials: Bank API tokens are stored in Azure Key Vault — a dedicated secrets management service with hardware-level security. They are never stored in the application database, never logged, and never transmitted to client devices
  • Authentication tokens: JWT access tokens expire after 1 hour; refresh tokens expire after 30 days
Important: Bank API tokens (e.g. your Monobank personal token) are stored exclusively in Azure Key Vault and are never accessible to anyone except the automated sync processes. They are never shared with your household partner or any third party.

7. Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user located in the European Economic Area, you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

7.1 Right to Access

You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.

7.2 Right to Rectification

You may update your display name and account information directly within the app.

7.3 Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten")

You may request deletion of your account at any time via Settings → Profile → Delete Account in the app. Upon deletion:

  • Your email, display name, and Google ID are permanently overwritten with anonymised placeholders
  • Your FCM token is deleted
  • All bank account links are removed; API tokens are deleted from Azure Key Vault
  • Your Google OAuth relationship is revoked
  • Historical transaction data you contributed is retained for your household partner's budget continuity, but is fully anonymised — attributed to "Former Partner" with no link to your identity

7.4 Right to Data Portability

Data export (PDF/CSV) is planned for a future release. To request your data in the interim, contact us at defalex808@gmail.com.

7.5 Right to Object / Restrict Processing

You may unlink individual bank accounts at any time to stop transaction syncing for those accounts. You may disable push notifications within the app or your device settings.

7.6 Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent (e.g. push notifications), you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at defalex808@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days as required by GDPR Article 12.
